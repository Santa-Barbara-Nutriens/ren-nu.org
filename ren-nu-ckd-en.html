Section 02

The Ren-Nu™ Program from Santa Barbara Nutrients is a guided program designed to improve kidney and metabolic health through science-backed nutritional changes. Originally developed for polycystic kidney disease, the program is now available for patients with non-genetic forms of chronic kidney disease (CKD).

Section 03

What's Included

  • 100% remote — participate from anywhere using your phone, tablet, or computer
  • ~1 month onboarding period followed by a 12-week structured program
  • Detailed curriculum of online courses and digital resources
  • Personalized nutrition guidance in one-on-one online meetings with a registered dietitian
  • Step-by-step meal plans and recipes for a kidney-friendly ketogenic nutrition approach
  • Learn to track your own biomarkers using connected health devices
  • Three month supply of KetoCitra®, a non-prescription medical food for kidney health
  • Grounded in published, peer-reviewed clinical research
Section 04

Is This Program Right for You?

  • You have been diagnosed with chronic kidney disease (related to metabolic health; non-genetic)
  • Your kidney function is in the early to moderate range (eGFR 30 or better; your doctor can confirm eligibility)
  • You are motivated to make meaningful changes to your diet and lifestyle
  • Your nephrologist will continue to oversee your care while you participate
