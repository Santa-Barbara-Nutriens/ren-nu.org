Section 02
How it works

Targeting PKD on two fronts

The Ren-Nu™ program pairs a kidney-safe, low-carb ketogenic diet with KetoCitra® from Santa Barbara Nutrients. Together, they reduce damaging microcrystals, correct metabolic imbalances, and support kidney resilience.

01 Metabolic pathways

Correct metabolic imbalances

A kidney-safe, low-carb ketogenic diet shifts how the body fuels itself, helping to correct the metabolic imbalances seen in PKD.

  • Kidney-safe, low-carb ketogenic nutrition
  • Ketone levels maintained with KetoCitra®
  • More energy in everyday life
02 Crystal formation

Reduce damaging microcrystals

KetoCitra® is a medical food developed by PKD researchers to reduce the formation of damaging renal microcrystals.

  • Lower oxalate absorption
  • Normalized urinary pH
  • Normalized urinary citrate levels
The result

Stronger, more resilient kidneys

By targeting both crystal formation and metabolic pathways, participants gain better control of their condition, experience improved symptoms, and work to slow disease progression. That is essential for long-term kidney health and well-being.
